# Audio pipeline

1. **Voice isolation** (`scripts/voice_isolate.sh <project> <speech sources…>` — camera files, voiceover carriers, CapCut voiceovers): DeepFilterNet3 on 48 kHz mono WAVs extracted from the originals → `work/voice/out/`. Proxies take it (`make_proxies.py --voice-dir work/voice/out`, or `work/voice/matched` after `match_loudness.py` when several mics or takes meet); `doctor.py` warns when a speaking source still has camera audio. Keep a copy of camera-audio proxies only if the user wants an A/B; the originals on disk are the fallback.
   - Softer variant if the voice sounds processed: `voice_isolate.sh <project> --atten-lim-db 25 <sources…>`.
   - Never: ffmpeg `afftdn`, `agate`, `anlmdn` — rejected after listening.
2. **Mix in Remotion**: speech from `<Video>` proxies at volume 1; CapCut voiceovers via `<Audio>` at the draft's volume; the bed via `MusicBed` (0.3 in the cold open, wordless pauses and the tail, 0.18 under speech ≈ 20 LU down, short ramps, an end fade — `reel/public/audio/lofi_bed.m4a` from `make_bed.sh <project> [seconds] <preset>` or the user's track converted to that name). SFX: `Sfx kind="tick|pop|done|whoosh|softwhoosh"` from `make_sfx.sh` (all synthesised locally), one kind of sound per kind of action, levels in `numbers.md`. Profanity: `bleep.py` (soft two-tone «boop», −18 dBFS); swears closer than 350 ms are merged into one bleep when the list is made (`cut_from_transcript.py`, `bleeps_from_timeline.py`). Music beds: `lofi.py` presets warm / night / bright / dusk, a different one per video.
3. **Loudness** (`scripts/render.sh`): measure integrated LUFS of the rendered mix, apply a static `volume=+X dB` to reach −16 LUFS, `alimiter=limit=0.84:level=false` for −1.5 dBTP, measure again; one corrective pass if it is > 0.2 LU off, and the render fails beyond ±0.5 LU. (`alimiter` without `level=false` auto-levels the output up by ≈ 1.5 dB — every reel before 2026-10-22 shipped at −14.5…−14.9.)
4. **Checks**: RMS in a speech pause (bed level) ≈ −32…−36 dBFS after gain; speech ≈ −16 LUFS. If the user says "music too loud", halve the bed level (−6 dB: `R.music_bed(under=0.09)`) rather than touching the gain.

## Voice bus for camera takes (event vlogs, raw takes with lav mics)
Halloween vlog, 2026-11-02 … 04 — numbers in `numbers.md` («Voice bus»):
- **Declip before anything.** A lav held at the mouth clips in the transmitter; after the render gain the flat tops crackle («колонки хотят спалить»). `voice_isolate.sh` and `dualmic.py` run `adeclip` first (input halved so the rebuilt peaks fit, gain given back as float).
- **Two lavs on L/R → `dualmic.py`.** Clean each channel alone (DeepFilterNet limited to `dualmicAtten` dB — full strength sounded hollow), then a Dugan-style automix: each channel's gain is its share of the smoothed power. A downmix first doubles the room noise and adds the far mic's late copy of every voice (comb filter, «из толчка»). `--test START DUR <clip>` writes L / R / mix / downmix for an A/B by ear.
- **`voice_mix.py` = levels + ambience + taming, before the proxies.** Every clip's active speech to `voiceTargetDb` (at most `voiceMaxGain` up), each segment nudged into [`voiceSegLo`, `voiceSegHi`] (± `voiceSegMax`), the camera's own sound under `"amb"` picture beats at `ambDb` kept out from under every word, `"raw"` segments where DeepFilterNet swallowed a voice, then `tame()`: a 3:1 compressor over target + `tameOverDb` and a 3 ms look-ahead limiter at `tameCeilDb` — so render.sh's static gain never drives its own limiter into the crackle. Output `work/voice/mixed/` → `make_proxies --voice-dir work/voice/mixed`.
- A vlog with no music: the event's own sound carries the picture beats; digital silence under a dance reads as a broken file.

## Action reel sound
No music in the file — the user adds the track in Instagram (`inputs/action-reel.md`); the render is voices + a few SFX at −16 LUFS. Sound only where something happens: the hit on the drop, a beep, the power-down at the end; never the same whoosh on every cut (whips are silent). `make_sfx.sh` makes three variants of whoosh / softwhoosh / pop / hit and `R.sfx` cycles them.
