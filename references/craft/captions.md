# Captions

- Transcribe with `scripts/transcribe.py` (GigaAM v3 + Silero VAD under the memory guard, word timestamps, Russian; for another language `--lang <code>` switches to Whisper). It assembles the exact timeline audio first — segments at their timeline positions (gaps silent), CapCut speed changes time-stretched, muted segments silent, CapCut voiceovers mixed in — so times are already timeline times and nothing muted gets a caption.
- Group with `scripts/make_captions.py` → `reel/public/captions/timeline.json` (Remotion `Caption[]` with `pageBreakAfter`). Defaults from `numbers.md`: pages ≤ 8 words, ≤ 3.4 s and ≤ 54 characters (two lines at 50 px; a longer page would wrap to a third line and climb into the card band), never breaking after a function word; 1–2-word pages are glued to the next phrase; tokens in silent ranges and after the speech end are dropped (a bare recognizer hallucinates «Продолжение следует…» on silence; GigaAM sits behind a VAD, the filter stays as a safety net); swears are masked with asterisks (`--no-mask` when the intake says leave them).
- Display with the template `Captions.tsx`: Montserrat 700, 50 px, white with `shadows.caption`, centred, bottom at ≈ 1520. A phrase followed within 1.4 s by the next one soft-swaps (4-frame overlap, opacities sum to 1, 6 px rise) — never fade to zero between two phrases, that reads as a blinking line; only a real pause gets the 9-frame fade, with the phrase held 0.65 s after its last word. The last page fades inside the reel instead of being cut off; a page that still exceeds two lines shrinks its font (min 40 px). No per-word colour, no bounce.
- Print the page list and read it: fix hallucinated or dropped words in `work/caption_patches.json` (`drop` tokens, `drop_at` with a time, `replace` by token text, `replace_at` with a time) — `make_captions.py` and `tl_words.py` apply it on every run, so fixes survive a re-cut; never edit the generated JSON. Typical issues: brand names (`assets/brands.json` holds the canonical spellings), names misheard in narration, a number spoken one way and written another — write what was said («полтора миллиона», «1,5 млн» only if the user writes it so). Fillers like «короче» stay: it is the author's voice.
- `drop` / `drop_at` match RAW recognizer tokens, before gluing: an interjection like «Уи-хи» arrives as « Уи» + «-хи.», so a drop of «Уи-хи.» never fires — drop both pieces with `drop_at`.
- Word timestamps double as the sync source for every graphic: `scripts/tl_words.py` writes `work/tl_words.txt` (frame + word, brands and patches applied, silent ranges skipped); `work/gen_reel.py` looks words up through `reelgen` (`craft/scenes.md`).
- **Rail and embed**: every spoken phrase rides the calm caption rail — fillers included (they are the author's voice), never a highlighted or popping word. A *peak* phrase may additionally be promoted to an **embed**: one big word or number in the picture — a title behind the person (`BehindTitle`, ≤ 1 per reel), a `BigFact`, a `Stamp`. Embeds are scarce and earned: at most one per chapter, never two on screen at once, never in adjacent phrases, at least one spoken beat apart. Embedding every key word is the common mistake. The caption rail stays where `layout-rules.md` puts it — captions never float over the content, cards and captions never fight (non-negotiable).
- Profanity: the one matcher is `scripts/swear.py` (shared by `make_captions.py`, `cut_from_transcript.py`, `bleeps_from_timeline.py`; it knows innocent look-alikes such as «себестоимость», «подъезд»); extend it there when a word slips through or a harmless word is caught, and run its self-test (`python3 -I $S/swear.py`).
