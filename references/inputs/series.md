# Input: a series (several episodes from one pool of material)

Use when the material is more than one reel can hold, or when new material keeps arriving for the same topic (a devlog).

1. `$S/new_project.sh <path> <episodes>` (path: the session's folder when the pool is there, SKILL.md §1.1) → shared `assets/`, `work/` (inventory, transcripts, isolated voice and carriers of the whole pool), `PLAN.md`, `NOTES.md`, and `ep1…epN/` with `assets → ../assets` and `work/{voice/out,new,vo,pk,broll} → …/work/…` links, so an episode's scripts find the pool's isolated voice (without them the proxies silently take the camera audio); `work/voice/dialogue.json` and the stem stay per episode — the gains depend on the episode's own ranges. Each episode is then an ordinary project: `setup_env.sh <path>/epN`, `scaffold.sh <path>/epN` (its own `reel/`, ≈400 MB of node_modules — check the disk).
2. `$S/setup_env.sh <path>` at the top level too (the pool is transcribed and isolated there). Ingest, inventory, isolate and transcribe the whole pool once at the top level; episodes read it through their links. Check that every proxy line of an episode says `voice`.
3. `PLAN.md`: a row per episode (topic, length, status) and per episode a beat table — source, timecodes, what is on screen, what is missing. Split by speech length (≈2:00–2:30 of speech per reel). When a beat is missing, write the line to record (`inputs/voiceover.md`) instead of stretching another clip.
4. Build episodes one by one; copy a good scene file from the previous episode into the next (`cp ep1/reel/src/scenes/X.tsx ep2/…`, never symlink source code) and parametrise it (the title takes the episode number).
5. Identity across the series: an episode already published can be fetched and measured for continuity (`fetch.py <project> <url> --as own`, then `ref_analyze.py` — `inputs/reference-reel.md`).
   Then: the same title component with the episode number, the same caption style, chapter tags restart per episode, a different music preset per episode, covers with one background colour per episode (`craft/covers.md`).
6. No references to other episodes inside a video (no "в прошлом выпуске" cards): the user links episodes in Instagram. "часть 2" in a title is fine.
7. **New material later** (next week's shoot, re-recorded lines): ingest it into the same `assets/` (the log keeps the old mapping), `inventory.py <path> <new folders> --name inventory_<date>` for a separate sheet, transcribe only the new clips, add episodes with `$S/new_project.sh <path> <N+k>` (existing ones are kept), update `PLAN.md`. Published episodes are not rebuilt unless the user asks. Camera clips from another day may reuse DJI counters: link them with `ingest.py --camera link` (suffix `b`) or pass them by full path to one episode only.
8. Covers for the whole series live in `<path>/covers/` (`public/covers/` with the cut-outs) and are rendered from any episode's `reel/` with `cover.sh <path>/epN <path>/covers/public <path>/out/cover_ Cover1 …`.
9. `NOTES.md` per series: per episode the files, the cut ranges, what was cut and why, the facts on screen with their sources, how to re-run.
