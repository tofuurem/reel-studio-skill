# Input: voiceover takes (narration over screens, images and b-roll)

Use when the user records lines to be heard over pictures: a devlog over screen recordings, an explainer over renders, or the gaps of a camera edit. A whole episode can be voiceover only.

**Writing the lines** (when the material lacks a beat): `assets/voiceover_script.md` with a table *file name → text*, 5–10 s per line, facts taken from the material or the user's own words (never invented numbers), written in the user's voice; plus how to record: same room and mic, two takes per line, one second of silence before and after, one line per file, names `vo_NN_topic`. The user rewords freely — the transcript of what they actually said is the truth.

1. **Bring the takes in.** `python3 -I $S/ingest.py <project> <files|folder>` → `assets/voiceover/`. Recorded in CapCut: the files are in `…/com.lveditor.draft/<draft>/audio_record/`; `capcut_export.py --draft <name> --out work/draft_<name>/` (never the default `work/`: it would overwrite the cut list) shows the user's trims on the draft's audio tracks — the same seconds become the ranges on the carriers — read them (a false start is usually cut off there) and look at the takes they did not copy (alternate wording).
2. **Carriers.** `$S/vo_carriers.sh <project> assets/voiceover/*` → `VO_CARRIERS_DONE`, `work/vo/<ascii name>.mp4` (dark frame + the take; Cyrillic names are transliterated) and `work/vo/silence.mp4` (`--silence <sec>` makes it longer). From here a take is a normal source: `voice_isolate.sh` on the carriers (not on silence), `transcribe.py --sources work/vo/vo_*.mp4` → `work/new/c<id>.json` (never `silence.mp4` — Whisper invents text on silence), `pauses.py` for ranges.
3. **Propose the episode** — beats with the take, the pictures for it and what is missing — and wait for the user's OK before building. Then **the plan** as in `inputs/raw-takes.md`, with `"files": {"vo05": "work/vo/vo_05_tripo_v1.mp4"}` and `"words": {"vo05": "work/new/cvo_05_tripo_v1.json"}` (the id is `clipid.py`'s `c` + name); wordless stretches (cold open, a breath after a punchline, the end) are ranges of `silence` with `{"mute": true}` (mute ranges are always exact). Many small cuts inside one take are fine — there is no picture to jump.
4. **Loudness.** Takes recorded minutes apart differ by several dB: `dialogue.py` (core step 6) levels every take on its used ranges and nudges each segment; proxies take `--voice-dir work/voice/out`.
5. **Pictures.** Every carrier frame is covered by a `Board` or a full-frame `BRoll` (`craft/motion-design.md`, `craft/layout-rules.md`). Overlaps at cuts: `craft/numbers.md`. Face zones are not needed for carriers.
6. **Captions.** The recognizer misreads names in narration more than in camera speech («из Tripo» → «стрипа»): read every page against the script, fix with `caption_patches.json` (`replace_at` for one occurrence of a common token).

Traps: `pitfalls/cut-and-timeline.md` (carriers, boards at cuts), `pitfalls/audio.md` (levels between takes), `pitfalls/captions.md` (voiceover misreads).
