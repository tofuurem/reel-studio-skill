# Captions and transcription

GigaAM transcript behaviour, caption pages, brands, patches (Russian goes through GigaAM; Whisper stays for other languages, and its items below still apply there). Each item cost real time on a production (2026-10-09 … 2026-10-21); read the file of the area you are about to touch.

- One- or two-word pages ("что", "типа, на") with pauses around them make the subtitle line blink; `make_captions.py` glues them to the next phrase and `Captions.tsx` holds a page until the next one if the gap is <1.4 s.
- A recognizer may split words ("Яндекс" + ".Облаке", "туда" + "-назад") — glue tokens that lack a leading space.
- Karaoke word highlighting was rejected ("акцент уходит на субтитры"). Calm phrases, ≤8 words on two lines, soft swap between phrases (see the soft page swap in this file).
- Brand names come out lowercase/cyrillic ("дот") — `brands.json`.
- Low-confidence words (<0.4) are usually fillers; check them against the audio context before trusting a caption.
- **Caption "blink" that survived the bridging fix** was the page switch itself: page N faded to 0 over its last 5 frames while page N+1 faded in from 0 — a brightness dip at every phrase change, worst where phrases are short (under a timer card). Fix in `Captions.tsx`: bridged pages overlap by `SWAP` (4) frames with linear fades (opacity sum ≈ 1) and a 6 px rise; only a real pause gets the 9-frame appear/disappear. Two-line pages (8 words / 3.4 s / 54 characters, the `make_captions.py` defaults) halve the switches.
- **Silent tails and quiet words**: Whisper used to hallucinate in silent tails ("Продолжение следует…" on the music bed); GigaAM runs behind Silero VAD and writes nothing where there is no speech, but it can still drop a quiet word after a long pause ("я стараюсь не… [делать]") — read the page list against the audio and patch the token text. Tokens after the speech end are dropped by `make_captions.py` anyway.
- `transcribe.py` used to transcribe every file in `reel/public/audio` — the music bed too (minutes wasted, hallucinated JSON). It now mixes only the CapCut voiceovers listed in the cut list into the timeline audio.
- **Narration misreads** (seen with Whisper; GigaAM also misses foreign brand names): «из Tripo» → «стрипа», «Итого» → «Год», «Ходьба» → «Сходьба», «делов-то» → «делал лофта». Every take needs a read-through against the script and `caption_patches.json`; one occurrence of a common token is fixed with `replace_at`, never with a global `replace`.
- **GigaAM v3 + Silero VAD** (replaced Parakeet for the map and Whisper for Russian captions, 2026-11-13): punctuation and numerals come with the text, ≈ 20× realtime on the CPU, ≈ 1.3 GB peak; word STARTS are good, word ENDS are last-token time + 0.12 s (a 40 ms token grid: ±0.1–0.26 s against a hand check) — never cut or bleep on a bare word end (`bleeps_from_timeline.py` pads −0.2 / +0.28 s; trim pauses with `pauses.py`). Foreign brand names come out phonetic («Tripo» → «трипо»): `brands.json` / `caption_patches.json`.
- **Three-line captions** (audit 2026-10-22): pages were capped by words and seconds only; 62–74-character pages wrapped to three lines and the block climbed to y ≈ 1340, into the board and card band. `make_captions.py --max-chars 54` splits them; `Captions.tsx` shrinks a page that still does not fit two lines.
- **The last caption vanished at full opacity** when the last word ended at SPEECH_END: the page's hold ran past its Sequence. `Captions.tsx` clamps pages to the sequence and fades the last one.
- **CapCut voiceovers had no captions and no `fw()` anchors**: they were transcribed to separate files that nothing read. `transcribe.py` mixes them into the timeline audio now.
- **Anchors on misheard words** (`fw("tripa")` in ep2) because `tl_words.py` ignored `brands.json` and the patches; it applies both now — anchor on the corrected word.
- **`replace_at` / `drop_at` match every token with that text within ±1.5 s** (hermes-worker reel): `drop_at [21.94, "Гермес"]` also dropped the next «Гермес» at 23.28, and `replace_at [58.22, "4", "4:08."]` also turned «4 минуты» (56.90) into «4:08. минуты». When the same word repeats within 3 s, shift the patch time away from the neighbour (21.5 / 58.6) so only one token is inside the window, and read the page list again.
