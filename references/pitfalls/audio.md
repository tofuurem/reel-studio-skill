# Audio

Voice cleanup, loudness, music bed, bleeps, levels between takes. Each item cost real time on a production (2026-10-09 … 2026-10-21); read the file of the area you are about to touch.

- `afftdn`/`agate` denoising in ffmpeg made the voice sound "unclean" — rejected by the user. Use DeepFilterNet3 (`voice_isolate.sh`): voice level unchanged, pause noise −6 dB, no artefacts reported.
- DeepFilterNet needs `torch==2.4.1 torchaudio==2.4.1` (newer torchaudio dropped `torchaudio.backend`). Python 3.11 venv.
- Single-pass `loudnorm` is dynamic: it raised the music bed in every pause until the user complained twice. Measure integrated LUFS, apply a static `volume=XdB` + `alimiter`.
- Speech off the DJI mic is ≈ −25 LUFS; target mix −16 LUFS. Music bed ≈ 20 LU under speech (procedural bed: volume 0.18).
- No licensed music on the machine: Apple Loops absent, CapCut cache tracks are CapCut-licensed only. Procedural `lofi.py` bed is the safe fallback; the user may drop their own file in its place.
- Music: `make_bed.sh <project> [seconds] warm|night|bright|dusk` — a different preset per video; the user still recognised the procedural bed as «такой же как всегда» (BRX promo) — offer their own track first.
- ffmpeg's `sine` source peaks at about −21 dBFS, not 0: `volume=-14dB` on it gives an inaudible −35 dB beep. `bleep.py` compensates (`--gain` is the real peak, default −18 dBFS). Verify with `astats` Peak level inside a bleep window after every change.
- `bleep.py` is idempotent (mute + mix), so it can be re-run on an already-bleeped file.
- Two copies of the swear regex drifted apart: v2 shipped with "нихуя" and "проебываемся" unbleeped (prefix "ни" and root "ебы" missing in one copy). The matcher now lives only in `swear.py` (shared by `make_captions.py`, `cut_from_transcript.py`, `bleeps_from_timeline.py`); after every cut, read the bleep list against the transcript.
- **Bleep sound**: the 1 kHz sine reads as censorship and pierces; `bleep.py --style soft` (two decaying partials, low-passed, −18 dB) reads as a device sound. Merge swears closer than 350 ms into one bleep.
- `swear.py` caught harmless words built from the same letters: "себе" (prefix с + root ебе) and, in the 2026-10-22 audit, «себестоимость» — merged with a real swear next to it into a 1.55 s bleep. It now has an innocent-word guard (себе-, небе-, побед-, обед-, рубл-, корабл-, подъезд …); self-test: `python3 -I swear.py`.
- `voice_isolate.sh` printed VOICE_DONE even when DeepFilterNet failed (no pipefail) and `make_proxies` silently fell back to camera audio. It now asserts one output per input.
- **Loudness between takes.** Voiceover files recorded minutes apart differed by 6.5 dB (−28.3 … −34.8 LUFS), camera takes sat in between. `match_loudness.py` (per-source static gain measured only on the used ranges) made the final sections land within 1 dB. Target −24 LUFS, not −20: at −20 the peaks already hit the limiter before `render.sh` adds its own gain.
- **The limiter raised every reel by 1.5 dB** (audit 2026-10-22): `alimiter` levels its output up by default (`level=true`), so the static gain to −16 LUFS landed at −14.5 (lofiroom ep2) and −14.9 (birthday). `render.sh` now passes `level=false`, re-measures, corrects once and fails beyond ±0.5 LU — read the printed final loudness.
- **Muted segments got captions and bleeps** (audit 2026-10-22): the timeline audio for Whisper was cut from the sources without looking at the segment volume, so a muted CapCut sliver or a plan `mute` range was transcribed. `transcribe.py` now writes silence for muted segments.
- **Crackle when the mic is at the mouth** (Halloween vlog v2, 2026-11-03: «колонки хотят спалить, хрипят»): the lav transmitter clipped (flat tops at full scale) and a shout sat 7 dB over the speech; render.sh's +9 dB static gain then drove its 5 ms limiter. Fix: `adeclip` before DeepFilterNet (voice_isolate.sh, dualmic.py) and `voice_mix.py`'s tame() (compressor + look-ahead limiter at −12 dBFS) before the render gain — the render limiter then touched 0.1 % of the audio.
- **Two lavs downmixed = hollow voice** (Halloween vlog v1): each voice reached the far mic late, the downmix comb-filtered it and doubled the room noise. Clean each channel alone, then automix (`dualmic.py`); full-strength DeepFilterNet also sounded «из толчка» — `dualmicAtten` 12 dB.
- **Gains too generous** (Halloween vlog v2): +18 dB per clip and ±10 dB per segment lifted mic hiss and DeepFilterNet artefacts; the camera sound under speech in picture beats was a hollow echo of every voice. Limits `voiceMaxGain`, `voiceSegMax`; amb is gated out from under every word (`voice_mix.py`).
- **The same whoosh on every cut** (Halloween reel v2, 2026-11-04: «на каждый переход одинаковый вжух — странно»): in an action reel the track carries the cuts; sound only on events. Elsewhere `R.sfx` cycles three variants per kind.
