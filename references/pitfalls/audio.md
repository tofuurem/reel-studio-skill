# Audio

Voice cleanup, loudness, music bed, bleeps, levels between takes. Each item cost real time on a production (2026-10-09 … 2026-10-21); read the file of the area you are about to touch.

- `afftdn`/`agate` denoising in ffmpeg made the voice sound "unclean" — rejected by the user. Use DeepFilterNet3 (`voice_isolate.sh` → `voice_clean.py`), limited (see the 2026-11-07 audit below).
- DeepFilterNet needs `torch==2.4.1 torchaudio==2.4.1` (newer torchaudio dropped `torchaudio.backend`). Python 3.11 venv.
- Single-pass `loudnorm` is dynamic: it raised the music bed in every pause until the user complained twice. Measure integrated LUFS, apply a static `volume=XdB` + `alimiter`.
- Speech off the DJI mic is ≈ −25 LUFS; target mix −16 LUFS. Music bed ≈ 20 LU under speech (procedural bed: volume 0.18).
- No licensed music on the machine: Apple Loops absent, CapCut cache tracks are CapCut-licensed only. Procedural `lofi.py` bed is the safe fallback; the user may drop their own file in its place.
- Music: `make_bed.sh <project> [seconds] warm|night|bright|dusk` — a different preset per video; the user still recognised the procedural bed as «такой же как всегда» (BRX promo) — offer their own track first.
- ffmpeg's `sine` source peaks at about −21 dBFS, not 0: `volume=-14dB` on it gives an inaudible −35 dB beep. `bleep.py` compensates (`--gain` is the real peak, default −18 dBFS). Verify with `astats` Peak level inside a bleep window after every change.
- `bleep.py` is idempotent (mute + mix), so it can be re-run on an already-bleeped file.
- Two copies of the swear regex drifted apart: v2 shipped with "нихуя" and "проебываемся" unbleeped (prefix "ни" and root "ебы" missing in one copy). The matcher now lives only in `swear.py` (shared by `make_captions.py`, `cut_from_transcript.py`, `bleeps_from_timeline.py`); after every cut, read the bleep list against the transcript.
- **Bleep sound**: the 1 kHz sine reads as censorship and pierces; `bleep.py --style soft` (two decaying partials, low-passed, −18 dB) reads as a device sound. Merge swears closer than 350 ms into one bleep.
- `swear.py` caught harmless words built from the same letters: "себе" (prefix с + root ебе) and, in the 2026-10-22 audit, «себестоимость» — merged with a real swear next to it into a 1.55 s bleep. It now has an innocent-word guard (себе-, небе-, побед-, обед-, рубл-, корабл-, подъезд …); self-test: `python3 -I swear.py`.
- `voice_isolate.sh` printed VOICE_DONE even when DeepFilterNet failed (no pipefail) and `make_proxies` silently fell back to camera audio. It now asserts one output per input.
- **Loudness between takes.** Voiceover files recorded minutes apart differed by 6.5 dB (−28.3 … −34.8 LUFS), camera takes sat in between. A per-take gain measured only on the used ranges made the final sections land within 1 dB — now `dialogue.py` (take gains + per-segment nudges; the stem at `stemLufs` −24, not −20: at −20 the peaks already hit the limiter before `render.sh` adds its own gain).
- **The limiter raised every reel by 1.5 dB** (audit 2026-10-22): `alimiter` levels its output up by default (`level=true`), so the static gain to −16 LUFS landed at −14.5 (lofiroom ep2) and −14.9 (birthday). `render.sh` now passes `level=false`, re-measures, corrects once and fails beyond ±0.5 LU — read the printed final loudness.
- **Muted segments got captions and bleeps** (audit 2026-10-22): the timeline audio for the recognizer was cut from the sources without looking at the segment volume, so a muted CapCut sliver or a plan `mute` range was transcribed. `transcribe.py` now writes silence for muted segments.
- **Crackle when the mic is at the mouth** (Halloween vlog v2, 2026-11-03: «колонки хотят спалить, хрипят»): the lav transmitter clipped (flat tops at full scale) and a shout sat 7 dB over the speech; render.sh's +9 dB static gain then drove its 5 ms limiter. Fix: `adeclip` where the source clipped (voice_clean.py, dualmic.py) and a look-ahead limiter in the voice chain before the render gain (now `stemPeakDb`, see the audit below).
- **Two lavs downmixed = hollow voice** (Halloween vlog v1): each voice reached the far mic late, the downmix comb-filtered it and doubled the room noise. Clean each channel alone, then automix (`dualmic.py`); full-strength DeepFilterNet also sounded «из толчка» — `dualmicAtten` 12 dB.
- **Gains too generous** (Halloween vlog v2): +18 dB per clip and ±10 dB per segment lifted mic hiss and DeepFilterNet artefacts; the camera sound under speech in picture beats was a hollow echo of every voice. Limits `voiceMaxGain`, `voiceSegMax`; amb is gated out from under every word (`dialogue.py`).
- **The same whoosh on every cut** (Halloween reel v2, 2026-11-04: «на каждый переход одинаковый вжух — странно»): in an action reel the track carries the cuts; sound only on events. Elsewhere `R.sfx` cycles three variants per kind.
- **«The sound gets slightly worse» — audit of the reel-studio reel, 2026-11-07** (quiet room, separate DJI lav). Measured against the camera track, every stage took something; the fixes are the current pipeline (`craft/audio.md`):
  - `adeclip` ran on every source though nothing clipped — it still rewrote the waveform (difference up to −34 dBFS). Now only around clipped samples.
  - Full-strength DeepFilterNet: pauses at −69 dBFS (digital silence: the voice «breathes» and sounds gated), −3.2 dB cut out of the voice at 3.15 kHz (it fought the insects there), −4.3 dB above 16 kHz. Limited to `noiseTargetDb − SNR` (18 dB on that take): 3.15 kHz −1.5 dB, pauses keep their room.
  - Three dynamics stages in a row (tame compressor + limiter, a 2.5:1 compressor, the render limiter): LRA 8.5 → 2.6 LU, noise and artefacts lifted. Now one compressor + one look-ahead limiter: LRA 5.1.
  - Four AAC encodes (camera → 192k proxy → Remotion 320k → final 192k). Now the restored voice stays float/PCM until one AAC 320k encode.
  - No EQ on a dark lav under clothing (energy peak 400–500 Hz, 2–5 kHz ~20 dB down): measured, bounded moves now (that take: mud −2.5, presence +2.3, air +3 dB).
  - Hard audio switches on 44 cuts (no crossfade): now 10 ms equal-power crossfades from source handles.
  - Sample-peak limiting let inter-sample peaks through (true peak −0.8 dBTP in the final). Now a 4× oversampled limiter.
- **`ffmpeg -ac 1` adds +3 dB to a stereo track with the same lav on both channels** (each channel at 0.707). The analysis saw «clipping» that was not there. `voicelib.read` averages the channels.
- **Speech off a lav peaks 18–23 dB over its loudness.** A stem limiter at −12 dBFS (PLR 12) limited 31–66 % of the time — audibly squashed. At −10 with the phase rotator first: ≈ 12 %, ≤ 5 dB on rare peaks.
- **`dialogue.ts` next to `Dialogue.tsx` breaks `tsc` on macOS** (case-insensitive file system: «differs only in casing»). The data module is `dialogueSrc.ts`.
- **Remotion writes PCM sound only into .mov/.mkv** (`--audio-codec=pcm-16` with an .mp4 name fails); render.sh's intermediate is `<out>.raw.mov`.

- **A handlebar action cam played its wind at the voice's level** (Halloween v4, 2026-11-08: Action 5 Pro, built-in mic, no lavs). A picture beat (`amb`) got the "restored voice" of its clip at the clip's gain — with no speech in the clip there is nothing to level by, so DeepFilterNet's leftover wind sat at ≈ −22 dBFS. `dialogue.py` v3: an `amb` beat with **no transcript word in its source range** plays only the camera sound at `ambDb` over room tone. The first try (v2: "no speech beat of the same clip in the cut") left 35 s of digital silence: alias clips `<id>x<k>` count as separate clips, and on lav footage the camera track IS the lavs, gated under every word — a spoken line went silent. Decide by words, never by clip ids.
- **DeepFilterNet leaves the first ≈ 0.6 s of a file silent** — a picture beat that starts at source second 0 of a lav clip had 0.7 s of digital silence (audio_qa). Start such a beat at ≥ 0.7 s.
- **`dualmic.py` re-cleaned every clip on every run** (fixed 2026-11-08): the saved windows come back from JSON as lists and never equalled the tuples of the new signature. With 51 takes a no-op re-run took ≈ 1 h. Also: alias clips `<id>x<k>` passed together with their take each cleaned all of the take's windows again — they now get a link to the take's voice.
- **A time-lapse ride played its own sound time-stretched ×12–×15** (Halloween v5 review, 2026-11-09: «как будто кадр перетёрт» — a grinding texture before the beach): `dialogue.py` v5 plays a picture beat sped up `ambRealtimeSpeed`× or more with its camera sound in real time from the beat's first second, bridges too.
- **A radio-mic dropout left digital silence in the stem** (c0081: 0.5 s of zeros in the camera file itself, at the start of the beach scene): `dialogue.py` v5 treats zeros longer than `dropoutMs` inside a take as a hole — room tone under the voice, and the camera sound of a picture beat or bridge fills its own dropout from the stretch just before it. `audio_qa.py` reports digital silence inside the speech; read it.
- **What a podcast edit learned that a 40-s reel hides** (ported 2026-11-13; one voice for an hour shows every flaw):
  - «Гудит» lives on a few NARROW peaks (one host: 108 / 214 / 320 Hz on one mic, 61 / 110 / 219 / 320 Hz on another) — a broad cut at 315 Hz thinned the voice and left the boom. `voicelib.resonances` / `notch_plan` find them; `"notches": "auto"` cuts them (Q 3, ≤ 4.5 dB). The «Покупка байка» stem has a hum at 61 Hz, +6.5 dB over the trend, that the default chain leaves.
  - A voice that drifts inside one take (distance to the mic) is not fixed by per-segment nudges: the slow `leveler` (held through pauses — no noise pumping) took LRA 9.9 → 5.8. It runs before room tone and only on the speech.
  - What the user called «эхо» was the room tail after words: the downward `expander` keyed by the voice's own level removed it; deeper than 15 dB sounded gated.
  - `transfer` / `ltas` on 1500 sampled frames were ±2 dB noisy at 5–8 kHz — enough to raise a false «moved > 2 dB» WARNING or hide a real one. They now sum every speech frame (exact).
  - Every one of these was decided by ear from an A/B of 30 s, not by rebuilding the episode — and one A/B winner still failed in context (`craft/ab.md`). All are OFF unless `work/voice_tune.json` asks: a project without it renders bit-identical (checked on «Покупка байка»).
