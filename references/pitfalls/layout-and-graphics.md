# Layout and graphics

Face zones, safe areas, fonts, components, facts on screen. Each item cost real time on a production (2026-10-09 … 2026-10-21); read the file of the area you are about to touch.

- Face position differs per shot: wide couch shot ≈ y 800–1050, medium ≈ 700–1100, close-up ≈ 500–1200 (1080x1920). Run `face_zones.py`; cards go in the free band (usually the top band y 300–700, below the chapter tag). For close-ups with big graphics use `Stage` (video shrinks to a top window, graphics below).
- Reels UI eats the bottom ~320 px and the right edge; captions sit with their bottom at y ≈ 1520 (`paddingBottom: 400`), nothing important below 1560 or above 180.
- Rotated stamps at 118 px overflow 1080 px width; 92 px fits.
- Fonts must have Cyrillic: Unbounded (display), Montserrat (body), JetBrains Mono — local variable fonts (`reel/public/fonts`), weights 100–900. None of them has ✓ / ✗: check marks are drawn (`Mark`), and there are no emoji in UI text.
- **`interpolate` ranges**: `fadeIn={0}` / `fadeOut={1}` produce non-monotonic input ranges — components must clamp (`Math.max`), see `BRoll.tsx`.
- Montserrat was loaded in 600/800 while captions asked for 700 and Unbounded in 700/900 while boards asked for 800 (Chrome synthesised the weight); the variable fonts cover every weight now.
- **Close-up talking heads** (selfie framing, face y 250–1300) leave no band for cards and the chin sits near the captions. `SplitStage` shrinks the take into a left window (scale 0.5, y 300–1260) and puts notes in a 404 px right column; column text under ~30 px was too small in the first pass — 33 px items, 64 px numbers.
- **Board content band**: y 300–1350. The chapter tag lives at y 200, captions at 1400–1520; a board that starts at y 240 collides with the tag.
- **Facts on screen** came from the game's own docs and files, each one re-checked before use (`atlas.json` listed 7 clips although the docs said 5; the PNG count gave "3075 frames"). When docs and files disagree, count the files.
- **White text on coral and aqua plates** was 2.6:1 and 1.7:1 (WCAG minimum 4.5 for small text); the birthday reel swapped colours by hand. Text on a fill follows `inkOn(bg)` / `onAccent` from the palette; the contrast table is in `assets/examples/README.md`.
- **Default positions landed on the face**: `Card` defaulted to top 880 and `Stamp` to 1040 — the middle of a medium shot. Both default to y 300 now; take the band from `R.free_top` / `R.free_bottom`.
- **Face zones ignored the punch zoom** (1.2–1.3 + drift around y 730), so a "free" band was partly covered by the zoomed face; the birthday generator corrected it by hand. `face_zones.py` maps the boxes through `work/timeline_zoom.json` — `face_zones.json` is in screen coordinates.
- **The centre crop lost the action** (Halloween reel, 2026-11-04): the kick at the right edge, «стоит фоткает» with one friend on the left and another crouching on the right, a girl in the shop — all half out of a 9:16 centre crop; placing each crop by hand from frame grabs took longer than the cut. `people_zones.py` (Apple Vision, no download) now decides the crop before the proxies and make_proxies refuses to guess. Two lessons from the prototype: «the biggest box» swaps between a standing and a crouching person every few frames (the crop swung between them — the subject is sticky now), and a static wide shot's biggest person is often not the action (a seated man in front of the one waving) — that is what the sheet and `cropX` are for.
- **Template scenes carried facts of old videos** (137 GB, «Газонокосилка», prices); they were taken out of the template — the kit holds no video's facts.
- **A title «behind» the person painted over them** (2026-11-05): the cutout video was an in-flow element and the absolutely positioned title painted above it whatever the JSX order. Every layer of a behind-title stack is an AbsoluteFill (`scenes/BehindTitle.tsx`). A word centred on the face hides its middle letters — the title goes where the top of the head crosses it (`title_place`), the kicker in front.
- **Headphones cut out of the person** (reel_studio_skill hook, 16:9, 2026-11-06 → fixed 2026-11-07: «очень криво вырезание головы из наушников»): Apple Vision person segmentation (and subject lifting too) left the over-ear cups out of the mask — the white title showed through them as a haze — and its low-res mask, upscaled and blurred, gave a mushy edge; the EMA over frames could trail when the person moved. `person_matte.py` now runs BiRefNet (MIT) on a fixed crop around the people (≈ 1000 px of model input even for a seated figure in 16:9), blends the previous mask only where the picture did not change, and chokes the edge (0.15) so no green fringe crosses a white letter. ≈ 0.9 s per 1440p frame on Apple Silicon.
- **The hook title sat on the ears** (same hook): the generator placed it «head top + 0.17» with the skill's `punchCenterY` 730 while the 16:9 project's template used 450, so the word landed lower than computed. Map mattes through the zoom the viewer sees, with the project's own numbers, and let `title_place` score the result. An optimiser without taste picked a shoulder edge that ate the «R» («EEL-STUDIO») — the first and last letters and «the head is what crosses the word» are hard rules in the score now.
- **Text too wide for the canvas** (2026-11-05): «ХЭЛЛОУИН» at 190 px in the wide display face ran off a 1080 frame; BehindTitle fits the size to ≈ 90 % of the width.
- **`face_zones.py` was 9:16-only** (hermes-worker reel, 16:9, 2026-11-06): on a 1920×1080 canvas it reported faces at y 1636 and no x, so `free_top` / `free_bottom` meant nothing. It now reads the cut list's canvas: boxes on 1920×1080, samples every 0.5 s, ignores faces under 60 px (a video call on the laptop), writes `faceLeft / faceRight`, `freeLeft / freeRight`, `side`; `R.free_side(a, b)` picks the side for cards.
