# Cut and timeline

The user's edit, cutaways, transitions, exact cut points, carriers, boards at cuts, synced windows. Each item cost real time on a production (2026-10-09 … 2026-10-21); read the file of the area you are about to touch.

- Transfer the cut list 1:1. Do not shorten speech. Preserve 0.3-second mutes (`volume 0.001`) — they hide clicks; `gen_timeline.py` merges them into a volume callback.
- CapCut voiceovers (`audio_record/*.aac`) belong on the timeline where the draft's audio track put them (`cutlist.tracks`, type `audio`, materialType `record`).
- The user may already have cutaways in the main track (laptop shot, pool shot). Check the contact sheet before adding b-roll on top of them — covering their cutaway with yours looks like a mistake.
- A cutaway must fully cover any CapCut cut it straddles: start ≥ 8 frames before the cut (fade-in finished) and end ≥ 19 frames after it (the incoming shot's 7-frame dissolve + the layer's 12-frame fade), dissolving *into* the new shot; `R.cutaway` / `R.board` widen to that automatically. Otherwise 3–4 frames of a different talking-head take flash through ("непонятный кадр с лицом").
- Do not place a cutaway that shows the same thing as the user's next shot (walk to the lounger → cut → already lying on the lounger → walk again).
- A cutaway must show a *different* framing. A snippet from the same static take (user never moved) reads as a jump cut, not a cutaway — check the contact sheet.
- **"Transitions too harsh"**: hard jump cuts. `gen_timeline.py --xfade 7` now overlaps every cut (outgoing clip held muted under the incoming one, which dissolves in; audio still switches on the cut). A segment with `"transition": "whip"` in the cut list gets a blur+slide whip (used when the speaker says "идём на другой ракурс"). Flash peak 0.85→0.42 over 16 frames; b-roll fades 6→12.
- **Cold open on a cutaway**: a `BRoll` with `fadeIn` lets the talking head peek through on frame 0 → `fadeIn={0}` + `ui/FadeFromBlack`. Let the cutaway *hold* through the timeline's own dissolve and fade out afterwards, otherwise three layers blend at once.
- **Trimming pauses / cutting inside a take**: transcript word ends drift ±0.1–0.3 s and assign pauses to the preceding word's end. Use `scripts/pauses.py <voice.wav> <start> <dur> [thr_db] [min_s]` (10 ms RMS envelope on the DeepFilterNet wav) to get real speech boundaries, then write exact seconds into the cut list. Bleep ranges are snapped to the voice-activity run around the word (`speech_run` in `common.py`), else half a swear stays audible.
- A CapCut transition arrives as a dict `{name, dur}` in the cut list; `gen_timeline` must not pass it as the `enter` prop (treat as dissolve). CapCut speed ramps need `playbackRate` on `Clip`.
- **gen_timeline `--punch 1.2`**: a wide pool/couch shot leaves the speaker tiny; a base punch of 1.2 (alternating 1.2/1.3) keeps the reels rhythm. A segment can override it (`"punch": 1.0` on the tail shot).
- **Voiceover as a timeline source.** The cut list, captions and `fw()` anchors all assume video segments, so an audio take becomes a dark carrier video (`vo_carriers.sh`). It then cuts exactly like a camera take (ranges at measured pauses; many small cuts inside one take are inaudible because there is no picture to jump). A silent carrier gives wordless stretches a place on the timeline (cold open, 2 s to let an action finish, the end).
- **Boards and b-roll at cuts**: a board ends ≥ 19 frames *after* the next segment starts and the next layer starts 8 frames *before* it; ending a board 2 frames before the cut showed the dark carrier through the cross-fade. Inside a montage use hard cuts: `BRoll fadeIn={0} fadeOut={0}` and start each shot 2 frames before the previous one ends.
- **Synchronised windows** (same action from three sources): measure the moment in each source (strip of frames every 0.5 s), then `trimBefore = moment·30 − lead` for every window. A `<Video>` inside a window that pops in later still plays on the sequence clock — do not add the pop-in frame to `trimBefore`.
- **A `mute` range without words vanished** (audit 2026-10-22): with `"exact": false` the cut took ranges from the word list, found no words in a picture-only range and dropped it — while exiting 0. Mute ranges are exact now, and an empty, backwards or sub-frame range stops `cut_from_transcript.py` with `PLAN ERRORS` before anything is written; `gen_timeline.py` refuses a zero-length segment.
- **CapCut speed changes** (audit 2026-10-22): proxies, the timeline audio, loudness ranges and face-zone samples used the timeline length `dur` instead of the source length `srcDur`, so a 2× segment got a window half as long as it needed and captions inside it were wrong. All of them use `srcDur` now.
- **The user's CapCut framing was dropped and a punch zoom added on top** (audit 2026-10-22): `gen_timeline.py` now carries the draft's per-segment `transform` / `crop` into `Clip` and adds neither punch zoom nor drift for drafts (`face_zones.py` maps faces through the same framing). Keyframed (animated) framing is not reproduced — `draft_summary.md` lists those segments.
- **Sound bites instead of scenes** (Halloween vlog v1, 4/10, 2026-11-02): every scene cut into 3–5 s quotes. The user wants the setup, the joke, the laugh: plan scenes as whole ranges, `splitPauses` 2.4 s only removes dead air.
- **Phrases cut in the middle, silent fragments** (Halloween vlog v2): ranges ended where the word search ended, and a lone «Я.» between two long pauses survived the split. cut_from_transcript.py now grows a range to the sentence edge (`snap`, printed as GROWN), drops one-word pieces and lists what is still cut mid-sentence (MID-SENTENCE). Parakeet sometimes stretches a sentence's last word over the following silence (22 s) — word ends are capped.
- **One proxy window over a whole long take** (Halloween vlog): c0080 used 70 s spread over 509 s → 509 s of 4K encoded twice. `splitWindows` makes alias clips `<id>x<k>` (symlinks in work/alias, the cleaned voice linked, the LUT map matches the source name).
- **Cuts «on the beat» that were not** (Halloween reel v1): the first beat tracker said 76.86 BPM for a 115.4 BPM track. `track_beats.py` (onset autocorrelation, period and phase refined over the whole track) found 115.3 BPM and the drop to 0.02 s; actiongen measures shots in beats on that grid and checks `sync=(frame, "drop")`.
